隐私政策

阳光之下 / Under the Skin

最后更新：2026 年 3 月

概述

阳光之下（Under the Skin）是一款帮助用户在旅途中进行隐私安全自检的工具类应用。我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

我们不收集任何个人数据

本应用不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息。具体而言：

  • 不需要注册账号
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号或任何身份信息
  • 不追踪您的位置
  • 不向任何第三方服务器发送数据
  • 不使用任何第三方分析或广告 SDK

数据存储

所有检测记录（扫描结果、风险评分）仅存储在您本机的本地数据库中，不会离开您的设备。您可以随时在设置页面中清除所有 记录。

购买信息

应用内购买通过 Apple App Store 完成。我们不处理也不存储您的支付信息。购买状态（是否已购买）仅保存在您设备的本地 Keychain 中。

权限使用说明

  • 摄像头：仅用于反光镜头检查功能，帮助您通过摄像头预览发现可疑反光点。不录制、不存储任何视频或 图像。
  • 蓝牙：仅用于扫描附近蓝牙设备，识别可疑设备。扫描结果仅在本地展示和存储。
  • 位置（使用期间）：iOS 系统要求扫描 Wi-Fi 设备时必须获得位置权限。本应用不记录或上传您的位置信息。
  • 本地网络：仅用于扫描同一 Wi-Fi 局域网内的设备，识别可疑连接。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不有意收集儿童的任何信息。

本政策的变更

如本政策发生重大变更，我们将在此页面更新内容并修改更新日期。

联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请发送邮件至：support@undertheskin.app

Privacy Policy

Under the Skin / 阳光之下

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Under the Skin is a utility app that helps travelers perform a privacy and security self-check. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data.

We Collect No Personal Data

This app does not collect, upload, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration required
  • No collection of name, email, phone number, or any identity information
  • No location tracking
  • No data sent to any remote server
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

Data Storage

All scan records (results and risk scores) are stored only in a local database on your device and never leave it. You can delete all records at any time from the Settings screen.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely through Apple App Store. We do not process or store any payment information. Your purchase status is saved only in your device's local Keychain.

Permission Usage

  • Camera: Used only for the reflection lens check, which helps you spot suspicious lens reflections via the camera preview. No video or images are recorded or stored.
  • Bluetooth: Used only to scan nearby Bluetooth devices for suspicious equipment. Results are displayed and stored locally only.
  • Location (While Using): iOS requires location permission to scan Wi-Fi networks. This app does not record or upload your location.
  • Local Network: Used only to scan devices on the same Wi-Fi network to identify suspicious connections.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the content on this page and revise the date above.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please email: support@undertheskin.app
